Medical Career & Biography

Dr. Vaishali Sangole graduated from the prestigious Mahatma Gandhi Institute of Medical Sciences (M.G.I.M.S), Sewagram, Wardha in 1996, and completed her Masters in Surgery (M.S.) in ENT in 2002. She is widely recognized as a leading otoneurologist specializing in vertigo, balance disorders, and deafness.

Between 2005 and 2008, she served as a Clinical Associate to the legendary Padmashree Dr. Milind Kirtane (recognized nationally as the father of modern vertigo management and a pioneer of cochlear implants) at the P.D. Hinduja National Hospital. Under his mentorship, she gained extensive experience in Cochlear Implants and Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery (FESS).

In addition to running her specialized clinic in Kharghar, she holds the position of Associate Professor in the Department of ENT at MGM Medical College and Hospital, Kalamboli, Navi Mumbai, where she guides postgraduate students and handles complex cases.
